2. What WorkBox is

WorkBox bundles small tools in a browser popup: clipboard manager, calculator, notes, tasks, color picker, password generator, converter, timer, stopwatch and Pomodoro, music player, text shortcuts, QR scanner and generator, image compression, an AI-answer blocker for search engines, and a website Protection feature. 4. QR codes

The QR test in the generator checks that a code can be read on your device. It does not guarantee that every scanner or camera will read it, so test important codes (printed material especially) before distributing them. Only create codes with content you have the right to use, and do not use logos or images you are not allowed to use.

5. Your content and your responsibility

  • Your data stays on your device. You are responsible for backing it up: use the export feature, because uninstalling the extension or clearing browser data deletes it.
  • The password you set protects the key that encrypts your saved clipboard texts. That key is kept in memory only, so one unlock is asked per browser session. Your password and your recovery code are never stored, which also means nobody can recover your texts for you: if you lose both, they stay unreadable. Keep the recovery code somewhere safe: it starts with WP and is required when you type it.
